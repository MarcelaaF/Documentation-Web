Why Limos

Most nutrition apps are built around Western meals. Limos starts from the plate in front of you: the chicken rice, the kaya toast, the laksa. Because eating well should not mean giving up the food you love.

About this project

The project behind Limos

Limos is being designed and built end to end by a four-person team as our Final Year Project — from requirements and system design through to a working Flutter app backed by Firebase. This site doubles as our project record: team reflections, meeting minutes, timeline, and documentation live alongside the pitch above.

  • Project title Developing App for Health and Fitness
  • Supervisors Mr Tian Sion Hui & Mr Terence Chew
  • Team Group 34 — 4 members
  • Module FYP-26-S3-34

Eating well, made easier.

Limos is built as a mobile app for iOS and Android, on Flutter and Firebase.
