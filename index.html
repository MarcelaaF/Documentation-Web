The health app that also feeds you.

Limos pairs meal, water, and weight tracking with a real recipe catalog — including dishes published by local health-focused businesses — so eating well is easier to start, and easier to keep up.

One app, three roles

Built for three kinds of people

Limos isn't just a tracker for one type of user — it's a small ecosystem, with its own space for the people using it, the businesses feeding them, and the team keeping it all running.

For everyone

Members

Log meals, water, and weight; browse the full recipe catalog; and track progress that actually reflects how you eat.

For food businesses

Business Partners

Publish recipes straight into the shared catalog and reach people who are already looking for what you make.

For platform oversight

Admins

Review new business applications, moderate the community, and keep accounts in good standing.

About this project

The project behind Limos

Limos is being designed and built end to end by a four-person team as our Final Year Project — from requirements and system design through to a working Flutter app backed by Firebase. This site doubles as our project record: team reflections, meeting minutes, timeline, and documentation live alongside the pitch above.
