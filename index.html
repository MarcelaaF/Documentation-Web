The product

Everything you need to eat well, in one app.

01 · Tracking

Personalised nutrition tracking

Know your targets, log what you eat, and see how the week is going.

  • Daily calorie and macronutrient targets from your profile
  • Food diary with meals, serving sizes and macros
  • Weekly and monthly progress reports

02 · AI-assisted logging

Snap it. Check it. Save it.

Take a photo or pick one from your gallery. LIMOS suggests the dish and its nutrition, and you stay in control.

  • Predicted dish, serving size, calories and macros
  • A confidence score with every estimate
  • Review and edit everything before it is logged

Estimates are a starting point, not exact measurements.

03 · Recipes & guidance

Recipes, and a nutritionist to ask

Find meals that suit you, then talk to a person when you want a second opinion.

  • Recipes checked against your diet, allergies and health conditions
  • Browse nutritionist profiles and send a chat request
  • Receive recipes written for you by your nutritionist

04 · Habits

Progress and reminders that keep you going

Small nudges and clear trends make consistency easier.

  • Weight and water tracking in metric or imperial
  • Meal reminders that skip a meal you have already logged
  • Your own reminders, with a logging streak

Made for Singapore

Your favourite meals belong in your health journey.

LIMOS starts from the plates you actually eat. Its food database is built around hawker and kopitiam staples, and its photo recognition is set up for Singaporean and other Asian dishes, so logging a meal takes a search or a snap, not a guess.

Trust & transparency

Honest about what the app can and can't do.

AI has limits

Photo recognition returns an estimate with a confidence score. It can be wrong, so every result is shown for you to review and edit before it is saved.

How personalisation works

Your targets come from the profile you enter: body measurements, activity level, goal, diet type and allergies. Recipes are checked against your diet, allergies and health conditions.

Privacy

Medical-history fields are encrypted before they are stored, access is limited by role through Firestore security rules, and you can delete your account.

Data sources

The local dish values are approximate typical-serving estimates curated by our team. Online recipe search and nutrition estimates use the Spoonacular API; photo recognition uses Google Gemini. LIMOS is not endorsed by, or affiliated with, these providers.

LIMOS is a student project and is not medical advice. Speak to a qualified professional about any health condition or dietary change.

Our story · FYP

The thinking and engineering behind LIMOS.

The problem. People who want to eat healthily often juggle separate apps for calorie tracking, meal ideas and professional advice, and most advice ignores their allergies, preferences and conditions.

Our answer. One mobile app that sets personalised goals, makes logging local food fast, recommends suitable recipes and connects members with nutritionists.
