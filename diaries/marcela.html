Conclusion

I’m not going to lie, working as a team turned out to be much harder than creating and building an application on my own. Throughout this project, I realised that there are many adjustments to be made, and everyone needs to be kept informed along the way. Communication must be clear and concise to ensure that all team members are on the same page. When even one or two members have a different understanding of the progress or direction of the project, it can affect the workflow and make it difficult to complete tasks according to the planned schedule.

I also learned that some features are more challenging to implement than expected, and we should have allocated more time to them during the planning stage. This experience taught me the importance of realistic time estimation, proper planning, and allowing room for unexpected challenges.

Even though there are lots of difficulties, working on LIMOS has been a really valuable experience. It was a great opportunity to work with people I had not known before, coming from different backgrounds and having different working styles. We had to learn how to adjust to one another, understand each other's approaches, and work towards the same goal. Overall, this project has taught me that teamwork is not just about dividing tasks, but also about communication, cooperation, patience, and being willing to adapt. These are lessons that I will carry with me into future projects.
