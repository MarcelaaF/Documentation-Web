Week 13 (25 September–1 October 2026)
Objectives Achieved
Added goal-aware recipe recommendations, calorie-range ranking, and scheduled meal, water, and custom reminders. Updated Firestore rules and added recommendation and reminder tests; the recorded test run reported 124 passing tests.
⚠ Challenges
Recommendations had to combine dietary and allergy safety checks with goal-based ranking, while reminders needed to stay synchronized with account state.
💡 Reflection
Separating hard safety filters from ranking kept recommendations useful without weakening restrictions. Centralizing reminder behavior made scheduling and cleanup more consistent.

💡 Reflection

Objectives Achieved
